FORKING AND PULL REQUEST NOTES

Fork:
A personal copy of another user's repository under your GitHub account.

origin:
The remote URL of your fork. You push your contribution branch here.

upstream:
The remote URL of the original repository. You fetch updates from this remote.

Feature branch:
A separate branch used to develop a contribution without directly changing main.

Pull Request:
A proposal to merge your feature branch from your fork into a branch of the original repository. Original maintainers review and decide whether to merge it.

Typical relationship:
YOUR_FORK:feature-contribution -> ORIGINAL_REPOSITORY:main
