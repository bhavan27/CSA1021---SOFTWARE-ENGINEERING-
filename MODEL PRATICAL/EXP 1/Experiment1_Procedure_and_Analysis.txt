EXPERIMENT 1: RAPTOR FLOWCHART – GCD AND LCM OF TWO NUMBERS

AIM
To design a RAPTOR flowchart to find the GCD and LCM of two positive integers using Euclid's algorithm.

PROCEDURE
1. Open RAPTOR and create a new flowchart.
2. Accept two positive integers A and B.
3. Assign X = A and Y = B.
4. Repeat while Y is not equal to 0:
   a. Calculate R = X MOD Y.
   b. Assign X = Y.
   c. Assign Y = R.
5. Assign GCD = X.
6. Calculate LCM = (A * B) / GCD.
7. Display GCD and LCM.
8. Run the flowchart with the two test cases and verify the outputs.

TEST CASE 1
Input: A = 48, B = 18
Euclid trace:
48 MOD 18 = 12
18 MOD 12 = 6
12 MOD 6 = 0
Output: GCD = 6, LCM = 144

TEST CASE 2
Input: A = 12, B = 18
Euclid trace:
12 MOD 18 = 12
18 MOD 12 = 6
12 MOD 6 = 0
Output: GCD = 6, LCM = 36

ANALYSIS: COMPARISON FOR 48 AND 18
Euclid's algorithm performs 3 remainder calculations for 48 and 18.
Checking every integer from 1 through the smaller number (18) requires up to 18 divisor checks.
Therefore, Euclid's algorithm uses fewer steps for this input.

RESULT
The flowchart logic calculates the GCD and LCM of the two given positive integers. The expected outputs are verified for both test cases.
